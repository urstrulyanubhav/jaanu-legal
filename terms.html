1. What the service does

Heyjaanu sends an automated private reply on Instagram to people who comment on your posts. By default, every top-level comment is answered. A workflow can instead be set to answer only comments that contain one of its keywords; that is a setting of the workflow, chosen when it is created and changeable at any time. It is your own Instagram professional account, and you remain its owner throughout.

It is run for you rather than sold to you. We build the workflows, run them, watch them, and report on them, rather than handing you a tool and leaving you to operate it.

You create your own account, and you authorise Instagram yourself: the connection is made through Instagram’s own consent screen, and no access token is ever typed, pasted or sent to us. One step in the middle is ours and is done by a person — we add your Instagram professional account as a tester on our Meta app, and you accept that invitation in the Instagram app. Until you accept it, Instagram refuses the connection.

That role is also why nothing further is needed from Meta. The app operates under Meta’s Standard Access, which covers Instagram professional accounts we own or manage and accounts whose holder has been granted a role on our app, and every account we serve holds that role. Advanced Access, App Review and Business Verification would be required only for an app usable by people holding no role on it, and this service relies on none of them.

3. What the service will not do

These are refused by design, not by policy:

  • No unsolicited messages. A message is only ever sent to somebody who commented on your post first.
  • No broadcast or bulk messaging. There is no such feature.
  • No reading of direct messages.
  • No messaging anyone who has opted out.
