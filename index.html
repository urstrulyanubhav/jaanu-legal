Heyjaanu

Automated private replies to Instagram comments. Somebody comments on your post with a keyword you chose; they get one reply in their inbox.

A message is only ever sent to somebody who commented first. There is no broadcast, no bulk messaging, and no reading of direct messages — those features do not exist in the product.
